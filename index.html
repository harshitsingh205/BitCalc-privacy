Data we collect

BitCalc does not collect any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, files, or any other identifying details, and the app has no account or sign-in.

BitCalc does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage analytics. All calculations are performed entirely offline on your device.

Offline by design

All features run locally on your computer. This includes:

  • The multi-radix programmer calculator and 64-bit bit grid
  • Bitwise and bit-shift operations and the expression input bar
  • The IEEE-754 float analyzer
  • The frame and protocol checksum suite (CRC-8, CRC-16, Checksum-8, Sum-16, XOR, Two's Complement)
  • The number converter, standard calculator and calculation history

Values and hex frames you type or paste are processed in the app on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Third parties

BitCalc does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking software, and it does not share information with third parties, because it does not collect any.

Microsoft Store

You download BitCalc through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft may collect information about your download and use of the Store under its own privacy statement. We do not receive personal data from Microsoft about you. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement for details.

Children's privacy

BitCalc collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age set by local law).

Changes to this policy

If BitCalc's data practices change, for example by adding an online feature, we will update this page and the "last updated" date below before the change is released.
